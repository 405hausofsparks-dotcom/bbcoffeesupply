Questions

Frequently asked

What exactly is private label coffee?

It's coffee we roast and package under your brand. You get a retail-ready product with your name, logo, and blend, while we handle sourcing, roasting, and fulfillment behind the scenes.

What's the minimum order to start?

We keep starting minimums low so new brands can launch without overcommitting, with room to scale as you grow. Exact minimums depend on your packaging and blend — we'll confirm them during your consultation.

Can I use my own bags or artwork?

Yes. Supply finished artwork, design it with us, or bring your own packaging entirely. We'll advise on what roasts and prints well so the final product looks and tastes right.

Can you develop a custom blend?

Absolutely. Start from one of our profiles or build something new. We'll sample and adjust until the cup matches your brand.

How long does it take to launch?

Timelines depend on blend approval and packaging lead time. Once your blend and artwork are signed off, we roast to order and ship promptly.

Where do you ship?

We roast in Moore, Oklahoma and ship nationwide across all fifty states — to your location or directly to your customers.
